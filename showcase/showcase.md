A rally in Carom is a fast duel across a dark, precise playfield. Read the angle, move your paddle into the shot, and send the ball back before it slips past your side. Every return adds pace, so a comfortable exchange can turn into a match point in seconds.

Your paddle's motion gives the ball spin. Sweep down through contact to bend its path one way; meet it while moving up to curve it the other. The flight keeps changing after the hit, rewarding a paddle placed for where the ball will arrive rather than where it is now.

Two fixed obstacles split the center into shifting lanes. Bank a shot off a bar to reach a corner your rival has left open, or let a curve carry the ball around the obstacle at the last moment. Each rally becomes a small puzzle played at full speed.

Choose Solo to test your reads against the AI, or Versus to face another player on the same keyboard. Race to eleven and keep a two point lead: at ten all, the rally that finally opens a gap decides the match.
